Try Nullify

Two small test pages that hijack clicks the way real ad and scam pages do. They're harmless: the only place they try to send you is example.com, a domain reserved for documentation.

Checking…

  1. Invisible layer over a video player: clicking the player tries to open a pop-up.
  2. Click anywhere and get redirected: clicking the text tries to send this tab elsewhere.

Try each page with Nullify switched off for this site (toolbar icon → switch), then on. Source: github.com/Aryavvij/nullify
